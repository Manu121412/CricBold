Purpose

CricBold is a simple web app designed to keep cricket fans updated with live scores, match highlights, and key game insights in one place. It brings fast, easy-to-read cricket information together for users who want to stay connected to the game without hassle.

Contributors

Mission

Our mission is to make cricket updates fast, accessible, and engaging for fans everywhere by delivering essential match information in a clean and simple experience.
